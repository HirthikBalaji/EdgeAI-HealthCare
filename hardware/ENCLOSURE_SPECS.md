## 2. Ergonomic Placement & Anatomical Landmarks

The patch is adhered diagonally over the **upper left sternal border (2nd to 4th intercostal space)**:
- **ECG Vector:** Aligns with cardiac electrical axis for maximum R-wave amplitude (> 1.2 mV).
- **PPG Optical Contact:** Interfaces directly over capillary microvasculature over the sternum.
- **TMP117 Clinical Probe:** Centrally positioned with thermally conductive silicone to measure true core-skin interface.
- **IMU Orientation:** Z-axis normal to chest wall for exact cough, posture, and vertical impact vector decomposition.
